Tag heuer Connected

From 2024 to 2026

For several years, I have had the chance and honor to work closely with Clement Bretin in his team for TAG Heuer Connected.

The years 2024-2025 were notably marked by the creation of the new OS design for the Calibre E5 watch. I had the opportunity to work within the product team and contribute my expertise across various UI design and motion design topics.

I am deeply grateful to have taken part in this exceptional project, which taught me so much and allowed me to grow both personally and professionally. A huge thank you to Jorge Sarquis, Clément Tabaries, Willy Pillard, Anthony Stephan Guilloteau and Julien Jouan for everything we built together.

And a very special mention to Clement Bretin without him, this journey would never have happened. He has been a true mentor to me.

All visuals and mockups presented in this video are the result of a collaborative effort within the TAG Heuer product team. While I contributed across all of these topics, this project is first and foremost the outcome of multiple iterations, discussions, and collective refinements.
