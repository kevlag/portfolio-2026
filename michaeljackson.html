The story of the king of pop

December 2023

On the occasion of the 40th anniversary of the iconic Thriller album, I immersed myself in the creation of an immersive biography in tribute to the legend Michael Jackson, whose music rocked my childhood.

I managed to unearth rare images capturing intimate and evocative moments of his life. This project represents several weeks of dedication and passionate exploration. Every pixel was designed to capture the essence of the King of Pop, bringing to life every chapter of his life.

Originally designed in 2023 as a visual concept and prototype, this project remained a design exercise for several years. Today, I finally brought it to life by developing the entire experience with Claude Code, experimenting with a vibe coding approach. Turning a project that had existed only as a collection of screens into a fully functional website was both a technical challenge and a way to rediscover the original vision from a completely new perspective.

This project draws on the wealth of information available on Biographie.com.
