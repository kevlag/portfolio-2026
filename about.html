I help brands, agencies, and startups
turn ideas into distinctive digital experiences, combining UX/UI & Motion Design, Art Direction, generative AI, and creative coding.

With over 10 years of experience in digital design and art direction, I've worked on projects for international brands including TAG Heuer, Peugeot, Ubisoft, Riot Games, PSG, Nespresso, Van Cleef & Arpels, Piaget, Cartier, Canal+, and Louis Vuitton, through agencies such as BETC Fullsix, AKQA, Dogstudio, Uzik, and Bonhomme.

Today, I combine UI/UX, Motion Design, Art Direction, generative AI, and vibe coding to turn ideas into distinctive visual concepts, interactive prototypes, and digital experiences.

I use AI both as a creative tool for visual exploration and as a way to rapidly prototype, iterate, and bring ideas to life.

Multiple award winner, with projects recognized by Awwwards and FWA and featured on Behance.
