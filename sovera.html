Sovera credit agricole

July 2026

Sovera is a consulting firm specializing in retirement planning in Switzerland and for cross-border careers between France and Switzerland. Its goal is to support clients in making the right retirement decisions, taking into account different pension systems, tax considerations, and their personal circumstances.

To support its growth, I designed the foundations of its digital experience. I created the foundations of a design system, designed the homepage, and developed several modular content blocks that can be reused to build future pages across the website.

The goal was to create a coherent, flexible, and scalable foundation that would allow Sovera to evolve its website while maintaining a consistent identity and user experience.
