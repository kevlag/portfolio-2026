Bobby Buddy

April 2021

Founded in 2015, Bobby Buddy is a French photographic duo based in Paris.

Bobby Buddy's guiding principle is experimental analogue photography to retain the essence of photography and combine the aesthetics of portraiture and fashion with a more plastic photography.
